The Sentinel client has explicitly rejected chamfered/hexagonal corners. Corners are sharp-or-round only.

**Why:** Stated directly by the client; it is a taste call, not a technical one, so it will not be re-litigated by argument.
**How to apply:** Never propose clipped corners, angled cuts, hex tiles or "tech bevel" shapes. The custom notched-card is the one permitted shape deviation.

Client complaints driving the 2026-10 redesign (verbatim):
1. "the graphical representation of companies is not done well, do things like a professional designer please" — the three-company org chart.
2. "the mobile view is also [bad] in everything starting with the main hero... remove everything that makes our ui AI generated".
3. Faint double vertical rules down the middle of sections (already deleted by the user).

**Why:** The client reads the site as generated/templated. Sameness of component is the objection, not colour or craft.
**How to apply:** Prefer deleting and differentiating over polishing. A section that repeats the previous section's skeleton is a defect even if each instance is well made. See [[sentinel-ai-tells]].
