Components identified (2026-10-11) as the "AI generated" signature on this site, in order of how loudly they read:

- The eyebrow pill: a white rounded pill with an asterisk glyph opening every section ("Our approach", "Who we are", "Our services", "How we work", "Next step"). ~8 per page, generic section-name labels.
- Icon-in-tinted-rounded-square at the top-left of every card, ~30 per page.
- Interchangeable link labels: "Read more", "Explore more", "More about us", "See all services", "Look at solar" — nine phrasings of "go".
- Uniform card skeleton: icon tile → heading → 2-3 line body → 3-4 chevron bullets → CTA.
- Every H2 written as the same cadenced "X and Y" / tricolon phrase of near-identical length.
- Perfectly even section padding (one value, every section) and one border radius everywhere.

**Why:** These are the stock components of generated landing pages; the client recognises the pattern even without naming it.
**How to apply:** When adding any section here, it must differ from its neighbours *structurally* (list vs timeline vs editorial photo vs figures band), not merely by background colour.

Org-chart root cause: Sentinel Integrated Technologies and Sentinel Security Services use near-identical green shield + wordmark lockups, so placing them side by side reads as the same company twice; Circuitron's logo is a low-res multicolour raster that breaks the green palette. The chart's problem is brand architecture, not layout — differentiate the three with colour fields and reversed white marks rather than relying on the logos to distinguish them.
