Capabilities

What this company does

Six lines of work, each with its own team and its own spares.

Protective asset management

Officers trained past the basics, posted to protect premises, assets and the people inside them, around the clock.

  • Round the clock manned security
  • Access and egress control
  • Residential and estate posts
  • Commercial and industrial sites
  • Incident response
  • Supervision and site visits

Event security

Order and safety at an event of any size, so the thing you planned is the thing people remember.

  • Access control and screening
  • Crowd management
  • VIP protection
  • Emergency response
  • Radio coordinated teams

Alarm monitoring and response

Breaches picked up in real time and a response team moving before you have finished the call.

  • 24 hour alarm monitoring
  • Rapid armed response
  • Real time breach detection
  • Patrol vehicles
  • Escalation to the control room

Dog units

A handler and a trained dog cover ground that cameras cannot, and deter what cameras only record.

  • Patrol dog teams
  • Site and yard patrols
  • Night deployments
  • Handler training
  • Support to static posts

Perimeter protection

Once the first line is breached the whole facility is compromised, so we build several lines, not one.

  • Layered perimeter design
  • Integration with CCTV
  • Integration with intruder detection
  • Integration with access control
  • Delay and response planning

Investigations

Led by officers who spent careers in the Criminal Investigations Department before they came to us.

  • Incident investigation
  • Loss and shrinkage enquiries
  • Security audits
  • Policy implementation
  • Staff vetting support
