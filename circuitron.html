What this company does

Four lines of work, each with its own team and its own spares.

Security systems

The same discipline the group was built on, sold and installed as a technology product in its own right.

  • CCTV surveillance systems
  • Intruder and alarm systems
  • Electric fencing
  • Video intercoms
  • Security system integration
  • Monitoring and maintenance

Communication and connectivity

Radios that reach across a farm and networks that hold up when the power does not.

  • Two way radios
  • Radio communication systems
  • Networking equipment
  • Wireless communication solutions
  • Structured cabling
  • Satellite and radio links

Automation and smart technology

Buildings that do the obvious things by themselves: lights, locks, gates, sensors, all under one app.

  • Automated gate motors
  • Smart locks
  • Smart lighting
  • Smart switches and sensors
  • Home automation
  • Smart security solutions
  • Integrated building technologies

  • Electronic equipment
  • Technology accessories

The ecosystem

Two businesses inside the business

Circuitron reaches the market two more ways: a shop and a recycling route.

CircuitStore

Connect. Shop. Innovate.

The retail arm. Security kit, electronics, radios, networking and computers, online and in store, with the support that follows a sale.

  • Online and physical retail
  • Product discovery and ordering
  • Payment and delivery
  • After sales support

EcoCircuit

Recover. Refurbish. Recycle.

The sustainability arm. Zimbabwe has little formal route for old electronics, so we built one: collect, refurbish what can be saved, recover the rest.

  • Collection and decommissioning
  • Sorting and refurbishment
  • Material recovery
  • Responsible recycling
